### Business idea:
- **Business Name**: Fishy Business
- **What we Do**: Sell fish
- **Products**: We work with fish
- **Who would Use Fishy Business?**: People who want to by live or dead fish
- **Purpose of the Site**: Buy fish

**Data we need to store**: Fish.
- For each fish: name, price, size, price per lb, status: live, dead, or filet, availability

### Business Idea Paragraph:
- The name of our business will be Fishy Business where you can purchase fish at a great price. The purpose of the business is to sell various different types of fish to a wide range of people looking to buy live, dead, and fileted fish. Customers who visit our website will have the ability to browse all the available fish in stock, and view all the information regarding each fish such as its name, price per lb, size, and status. Customers will have the ability to view images of each fish type, and the option to purchase the chosen fish through our online checkout system.
