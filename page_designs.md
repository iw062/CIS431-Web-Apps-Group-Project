# **Website Page Designs**

## **Home Page**
![Home Page](PageDesigns/home.png)
1. Welcome message and short explanation of the store.
2. Featured fish and a prominent Shop Fish button.
3. Business name/logo and navigation: Home, Store, Login, Cart.

## **Store / Products Page** 
![Products Page](PageDesigns/store_page.png)
1. A grid of available fish with photos, names, prices, and availability status.
2. Filters for fish condition: live, dead (whole), or filleted. 
3. Each listing links to the fish/product details page.

## **Product Details/ Purchase Page**
![Product Details](PageDesigns/product_details.png)
1. A large fish imsage with a very detailed product information page.
2. We would have the name, size, condition, availability, price/ price per pound.
4. Also have a quantity selector and an add to cart and purchase action.

## **Login Page**
![Login Page](PageDesigns/login..png)
1. Email and passord field to enter with a sign-in button and registration link.
2. Customers have the option to sign in to track purchases and save things in the cart to come back to later. 
3. Designated administrators have access to management pages.

# **Administrator Pages**

## **Admin Dashboard**
![Admin Dashboard](PageDesigns/admin_dashboard.png)
1. View recent purchases and orders including order numbers, customer details, total, date, and status.
2. Summary of available stock/ low-stock fish.
3. Navigation to stock management as well as add products.

## **Add/ Edit Product**
![Edit Products](PageDesigns/admin_add_product.png)
1. Some form to enter to update any kind of detail regarding the fish.  
2. Availability indicator and a Save product button readily available.
3. Option to edit fish or mark whether they are unavailable.
